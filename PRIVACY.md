# Deal Flash privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-24.

Deal Flash runs entirely in your browser. It has no server, no account and no
analytics, and it makes no network requests of its own.

**What it reads.** When it is active on a page, Deal Flash reads that page's
product names, prices, links and images to show them in its own card on the
same page. None of this is sent anywhere.

**What it stores.** The items you star (name, price, link) and your choice of
card view or page view are saved in your browser with `chrome.storage.local`.
They stay on your device and are removed when you unstar them or uninstall the
extension.

**What it shares.** Nothing. Deal Flash does not sell, transfer or share any
data. It does not add or change affiliate codes, links or cookies; a buy link
opens exactly the address the page gave it.

**Permissions.** Access to a site beyond the built-in ones is requested only
when you turn on "Always on" for that site, and you can withdraw it at any
time from the popup or the browser's extension settings.

**Limited Use.** Deal Flash's use of information complies with the Chrome Web
Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

**Contact.** Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
